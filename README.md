# Bismillah Medical and Veterinary Store

Production-ready, 100% offline-first Android application for **Bismillah Medical and Veterinary Store** management. Engineered for pharmacy & medical store owners to track sales, expenses, expiry reminders, customer credit ledgers (Udhar), and instant 1-tap WhatsApp statement receipts.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 100% Offline-First & Free
- **Local SQLite Database**: Built with Drift (Type-Safe SQLite ORM).
- **Zero Internet Requirement**: Works 100% offline without mandatory cloud subscriptions, Firebase dependencies, logins, or paid backend APIs.

### 2. Expiry Alerts & Reminders
- **Custom Expiry Thresholds**: Configure alerts for 1, 2, 3, or 6 months before expiration.
- **Customizable Reminder Schedule**: Daily reminders with custom notification sounds until marked resolved.

### 3. Simplified Daily Sales & Expenses
- **Daily Sales**: Title/Notes, Date, PKR Amount, Cash/Credit, Customer Selection, and Multiple Photo Proof Attachments.
- **Daily Expenses**: Title/Notes, Date, PKR Amount, Optional Category, and Multiple Photo Proof Attachments.
- **30-Day Recycle Bin (Trash)**: Soft-deletion for all deleted sales, expenses, medicines, and ledger records with 1-tap Undo/Restore capability.

### 4. Customer Credit Ledger (Udhar) & 1-Tap WhatsApp Receipts
- **Customer Ledger**: Real-time balance calculation, date range filtering, and payment tracking.
- **Pre-formatted WhatsApp Messages**: 1-tap launch with exact bilingual pre-filled WhatsApp receipts for Credit added and Payment received.

### 5. Document Attachments & Data Backup
- Store photos of receipts, invoices, paper records, and prescriptions.
- Full offline ZIP export and import backup tool in Settings.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Flutter / Dart
- **State Management**: Riverpod
- **Database**: SQLite / Drift
- **Routing**: GoRouter
- **Messaging & Sharing**: URL Launcher & Share Plus
